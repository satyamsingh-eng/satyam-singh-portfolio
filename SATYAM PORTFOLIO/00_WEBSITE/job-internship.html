03 / PROOF PACKS

Send evidence, not a generic resume.

Every serious conversation should receive one compact proof pack matched to the role lane.

PACK A / SYSTEMS
SARVAX execution layer

One page: workflow architecture, agent roles, tool gating, approval points, evaluation, and the business outcome.

PACK B / TRANSLATION
Founder-to-system case

Show how you listened to an ambiguous need, found the actual gap, structured the work, and shipped a web or operating surface.

PACK C / TRUST
QA + threat evidence

Show test design, bug/risk framing, threat research, verification logic, and how you make systems safer to use.

PACK D / RANGE
Leadership + writing

Use student leadership, long-form writing, and research to prove discipline, communication, and sustained independent execution.

04 / RELOCATION TEST

Japan first. Singapore in parallel.

The country is a work experiment before it becomes a degree decision.

JAPAN / PRIMARY
High-fit startup route

SINGAPORE / CONTROL
English-first market test

Strong fintech, compliance, payments, and wealthtech relevance. Employer-sponsored work authorization and cost must be checked early.

RULE

Confirm the exact role, language environment, salary, work pass, manager, and relocation support before committing money.
