## Curation Rule

An item is retained only when an official source names at least one concrete support item:

- tuition waiver or fee offset
- stipend or salary
- travel/airfare
- housing/relocation
- health insurance
- visa/work-authorisation support

Items with only an international invitation, a generic employer brand, an old deadline, or an Apollo record without official support proof are excluded from the qualifying route list.

## Country Findings

### Estonia
The national foreign-student scholarship is only €350/month and requires an admission confirmation by the 1 September annual deadline; India appears in Group B of the official guideline. The Startup/Scale-up Visa talent route is a real work-authorisation mechanism for a qualified employer, not a stipend or standalone job.

### Japan
MEXT is the best full-support Master's route but the 2027 India application window is not presented as open for new applicants on 28 August. OIST is the strongest immediate test because its support package is explicit and its Spring 2027 deadline is 15 October.

### UAE
MBZUAI is a high-fit AI institution with a strong stated benefits package, but the current admissions page qualifies M.Sc. funding by faculty/government funding. Khalifa University provides a Master's tuition waiver route, but the current postgraduate page says there is no Master's stipend.

### Germany
DAAD's general Master's scholarship is the most actionable current route. It provides a meaningful living and mobility package but explicitly does not cover tuition. EPOS is retained only as a course-specific watchlist because experience and course gates vary.

### Netherlands
Twente's UTS is actionable for September 2027, with €3,000–€22,000 for one year. Radboud's 2026/27 deadline has passed; its tuition reduction, visa, residence and insurance support are retained as a next-cycle watchlist, not an active deadline.

### Singapore
NUS and NTU research scholarships pass the hard support gate. They are not the same as coursework Master's routes: both require research/supervisor fit and programme-specific admissions. NTU offers the clearer dated August 2027 window.

### Russia
Open Doors is currently actionable and technically aligned, with tuition-free admission and a monthly stipend. It does not cover travel, accommodation or insurance on the official public page. Skoltech is a supported next-cycle watchlist with exact 2027 terms pending.

## Apollo Boundary

Apollo Composio organization enrichment was used for identity/domain, location and approximate employee-count cross-checks. Apollo was not used to prove:

- a live vacancy
- a scholarship
- a visa or relocation package
- a salary
- a hiring decision

Employer records without official support evidence were excluded from the qualifying route list. The raw Apollo payloads remain in the prior research directory; this report uses only compact identity signals.

- Native currency figures are shown without hardcoded INR conversion.
