02 — Why this matters

People don't miss opportunities because they're weak. They miss them because they found out late.

The radar exists to flip that. It sorts the next seven months into four windows so that at any moment, we both know exactly what's closing soon and what's worth preparing. The expensive mistakes — a missed Mannheim window, a half-filed scholarship — are structural, not talent problems. Structure is the part we control.
