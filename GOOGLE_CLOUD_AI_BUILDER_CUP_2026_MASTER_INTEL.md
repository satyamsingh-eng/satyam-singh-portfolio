## 5. PROTOTYPE CONCEPTS OPTIMIZED FOR GOOGLE CLOUD

To win a Google Cloud hackathon, your prototype must tightly integrate **Google's core GenAI ecosystem**:
- **Gemini 1.5 Pro / Flash** (Long context window, multimodal video/audio/document reasoning).
- **Vertex AI Search & Conversation** (Grounding with Google Search, enterprise vector search).
- **Function Calling & Agentic DAGs** (Structured tool calling, human-in-the-loop gates).
- **Cloud Run / BigQuery** (Serverless execution and data analytics).

### Concept 1: ApexTrade AI — Autonomous Cross-Border Trade & Compliance Agent
* **The Problem:** Emerging market exporters and SMEs lose weeks and face massive penalties navigating complex bilateral tariffs, customs documentation, foreign exchange volatility, and letter-of-credit compliance.
* **The Solution:** A multimodal Vertex AI agent that ingests invoices, shipping bills, and multi-country trade regulations; automatically checks Harmonized System (HS) code compliance; hedges forex exposure; and flags supply chain bottlenecks.
* **Team Synergy:** Leverages Shravani’s top honors in *International Business Operations* (89/100) and *International Banking* (83/100) alongside Satyam’s multi-agent DAG architecture at C3A Labs.

### Concept 2: AuraWealth — Multilingual High-Trust Wealth Management Copilot
* **The Problem:** Retail investors in Asia-Pacific face massive advisory barriers due to financial jargon, lack of trust, and language barriers. Financial planners spend 60% of their time on repetitive intake and portfolio updates.
* **The Solution:** A voice- and text-enabled multilingual wealth advisory agent that ingests client financial goals, parses mutual fund fact sheets and tax rules, and generates clear conversational explanations in English, Hindi, and regional languages.
* **Team Synergy:** Grounded directly in Shravani’s marketing internship at Aanshi Associates and Satyam’s wealth management architecture on SARVAX.

### Concept 3: BioBridge — AI Clinical Health Informatics Triage
* **The Problem:** Healthcare clinics and diagnostic labs struggle to translate complex clinical test data, pathology reports, and patient queries into actionable, empathetic patient plans.
* **The Solution:** An agentic healthcare triage system grounded in verified clinical research literature that assists physicians and helps patients understand care pathways.
* **Strategic Benefit:** Direct alignment with **Dr. Ali at NUS iHealthtech** and Satyam’s ArtRX project!
