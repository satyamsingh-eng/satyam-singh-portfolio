Read exclusions and limitations

Method boundary: The retained list is not a guarantee of admission, employment, visa issuance or financial sufficiency. It is an evidence-filtered research shortlist as of 28 August 2026.

Research sources: Official government, university and programme pages were preferred. Mixed web searches were used for discovery. Playwright/browser scraping was used where pages were dynamic. Apollo Composio enrichment was used for organization identity cross-checks. No applications, emails or messages were sent.
