## 2. TOP TIER SINGAPORE INSTITUTIONS & TARGET PROGRAMS

### Tier 1: Prime Target Programs (Optimal Fit for Freshers)

#### 1. Singapore Management University (SMU) — Lee Kong Chian School of Business
- **Program:** **Master of Science in Management (MiM)**
- **Why It Fits:** Ranked among the top 100 globally by Financial Times; engineered specifically for graduates with 0–2 years of experience.
- **Specializations:** Digital Transformation, Marketing, Operations & Global Supply Chain.
- **Intake:** August (Application cycles open Oct/Nov preceding year).
- **Admissions Criteria:** Good undergraduate degree (her 8.36/8.30 Sem SGPA is competitive), GMAT / GRE or SMU Admissions Test, video interview, essays.
- **Key Edge for Shravani:** Her demonstrated leadership as Class Representative, festival anchor, and marketing internship at Aanshi Associates.

#### 2. ESSEC Business School — Asia-Pacific Campus (Singapore)
- **Program:** **Master in Strategy & Management of International Business (SMIB)**
- **Why It Fits:** Ranked **#2 globally** by Financial Times (MiM/Strategy). Features a dedicated Singapore campus at One-North.
- **Curriculum:** Global trade strategy, corporate development, Asian business environments, consulting projects.
- **Direct Link to BBA:** Seamless continuation of her undergraduate coursework in *International Business Laws* and *International Banking Operations*.

#### 3. National University of Singapore (NUS) — NUS Business School
- **Program:** **M.Sc. in Management (MiM)** / **M.Sc. in Marketing Analytics and Insights**
- **Why It Fits:** Ranked #1 in Asia (QS World University Rankings). Pre-experience degree open to freshers.
- **Dual Degree Option:** CEMS Master in International Management (allows a semester exchange across global top business schools like Bocconi, HEC Paris, St. Gallen).
- **Application Window:** October to January.

#### 4. Nanyang Technological University (NTU) — Nanyang Business School
- **Program:** **M.Sc. in Marketing Science** / **M.Sc. in Business Analytics**
- **Why It Fits:** Strong quantitative and strategic focus on consumer insights and digital brand storytelling in Asia-Pacific markets.
- **Intake:** August.
