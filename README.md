## Portfolio thesis

Satyam’s work is not defined by one narrow title. The recurring ability is to absorb founder, client, user, and technical context; identify the highest-impact gap; turn it into an executable system; validate it; and stay close to the outcome.

The public portfolio reflects that center through:

- AI systems and multi-agent workflow work at C3A Labs / SARVAX
- Product and operations translation
- QA, evaluation, reliability, and security thinking
- Cyber Shell threat research and defensive product exploration
- Lunar Pixels web and digital systems work
- BCA Cloud Computing and Cyber Security education
- Concurrent business and venture-operations education
- Student leadership, published research, and long-form writing

## Public / private boundary

The public site contains curated work, safe-to-share evidence, official links, and research limitations. The ignored `PRIVATE/` folder preserves local-only material:

- Resumes and CV variants
- SOP, transcripts, and recommendation letters
- Raw Apollo enrichment and outreach research
- Research scripts and QA screenshots
- Legacy portfolio versions and generated study outputs

Private contact data is intentionally not exposed in the public roadmap. Public company dossiers show names, roles, LinkedIn routes, and verification status only.

## Design and interaction

- White, black, and neutral-gray editorial system
- Shared sticky navigation and reading-progress indicator
- Responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile
- Keyboard-visible focus states and accessible progress semantics
- Reveal-on-scroll with reduced-motion support
- Local checklist persistence for preparation work
- Cross-navigation between both tracks and all research reports
- No application, message, or external record is sent by the site
